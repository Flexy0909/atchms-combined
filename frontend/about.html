About the System

Modernising student accommodation

The Arusha Technical College Hostel Management System (ATCHMS) was designed to replace manual, paper-based hostel registration at ATC with an efficient digital workflow. It connects students, wardens and the dean of students through a unified interface accessible from any device.

By centralising applications, room data and notifications, ATCHMS reduces administrative bottlenecks and ensures every student receives transparent, fair treatment when allocating accommodation.

Hostel services we manage

ATCHMS coordinates the full lifecycle of student accommodation — from the day a student submits an application to the day they check out at the end of their studies.

Core services (ordered):
  1. Online hostel application and review
  2. Room allocation by programme & academic year
  3. Hostel fee tracking and payment receipts
  4. Maintenance ticketing and resolution
  5. Check-in and check-out management
  6. Reporting and analytics for administrators
