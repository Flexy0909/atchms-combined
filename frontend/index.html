I am a Computer Science student at Arusha Technical College (ATC). This website — the ATC Hostel Management System (ATCHMS) — is my individual project, built to modernise how students apply for, manage and track their hostel accommodation on campus using pure HTML and CSS.

The system replaces paper-based hostel registration with an efficient digital workflow, giving students real-time access to their applications, room details, payments and maintenance requests — all from one place.
